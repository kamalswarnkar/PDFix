Frequently Asked Questions

Is tryPDF! free to use?

Yes. All tryPDF! tools are completely free to use online with no signup required.

Are my files secure?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and deleted as soon as your download starts. Nothing is kept.

Do you store uploaded files?

No. Uploads are deleted straight after processing, and results are deleted as they are downloaded.

Do these tools work on mobile?

Yes. tryPDF! tools work on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
